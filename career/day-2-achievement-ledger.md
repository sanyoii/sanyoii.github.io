## Evidence classes

| Class | Public interpretation |
|---|---|
| Reproducible | Can be checked from a current public source or recomputed from disclosed inputs. |
| Date-derived | Can be calculated from accepted employment dates without implying business impact. |
| Qualitative | Direct work is supported, but the magnitude cannot be independently recomputed. |
| Sanitized | The work involved confidential systems; only generalized product flow, action, and risk may be published. |
| Excluded | The claim depends on unsupported percentages, scale, causality, or counterfactual impact. |

## Excluded public metrics

The public portfolio does not use:

- historical exchange-volume figures without a dated employment-period source;
- beta scale expressed as unique users when the remembered scope used deployment seats;
- support-volume or turnaround percentages whose population or formula cannot be reconstructed;
- deployment-time, operating-cost, or coverage percentages without accessible before／after data;
- customer, trainee, or participant totals without a reproducible count;
- hypothetical financial loss or downtime prevented;
- absolute statements such as all test cases automated without release、test-level and count evidence.
