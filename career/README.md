### 公開原則

- 不公開家庭健康、關係或其他私人生活細節。
- 不公開前公司的內部文件、客戶資料、交易資料或系統實作資訊。
- 無法重算或獨立查驗的百分比與規模數字，不作為公開成果。
- Responsibilities 與 impact 分開；團隊成果不改寫成個人單獨成果。
- Crypto Exchange 案例只保留去識別化的產品流程與品質風險。

### Publication rules

- Exclude private family, health, relationship, and personal-life details.
- Exclude former-employer documents, customer data, transaction data, and implementation details.
- Do not publish percentages or scale claims that cannot be independently checked or recomputed.
- Keep responsibilities separate from impact and team outcomes separate from individual contribution.
- Keep crypto-exchange examples sanitized and limited to product flows and quality risks.
