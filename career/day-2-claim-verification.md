## Prohibited transformations

- Do not convert deployment seats into unique users.
- Do not combine or repeat support-improvement claims from different Trend Micro roles without distinct periods and populations.
- Do not describe all QA tenure as final-escalation tenure.
- Do not present six engineers plus the project leader as a six-person total team.
- Do not change `Senior Automation Test Engineer` into `Solutions Engineer`.
- Do not substitute a current exchange-volume snapshot for historical employment-period evidence.
- Do not describe confidential defect discovery as a verified amount of loss prevented.
- Do not expand the confirmed weekday 09:00–24:00 Taiwan schedule into 24-hour availability or coverage of every European timezone.

- [x] Former-employer evidence sources and implementation details excluded.
- [x] Unsupported percentages and scale metrics excluded.

- [x] Crypto-exchange defects reduced to sanitized product-flow categories.
