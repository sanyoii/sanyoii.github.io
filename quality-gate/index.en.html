Severity definitions Draft

  • S1 Critical: The critical path is unusable with no workaround; or data corruption/loss, payment or points errors (double charges, points deducted), or a security or privacy leak. These three categories are always S1, regardless of whether a workaround exists or a refund can remedy it afterward. Representative cases: cannot log in or complete payment; task data overwritten and lost; other people’s data leaked
  • S2 Major: A main feature is broken or gives wrong results; a difficult workaround exists. Representative cases: report values miscalculated but can be recalculated manually; a main flow can only be completed via a detour
  • S3 Minor: A secondary feature or boundary error; a simple workaround exists. Representative cases: wrong boundary value in a filter condition; incorrect validation message on a secondary field
  • S4 Trivial: Text, layout, or appearance; does not affect functionality. Representative cases: typos, misalignment, inconsistent colors

Deferring a fix does not lower severity; QA suggestions and client rulings are recorded separately.
